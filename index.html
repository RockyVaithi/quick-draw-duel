How to play
1. Everyone watches the screen. It turns yellow (HOLD) to trick you — don't tap.
2. When it turns green (DRAW!), tap anywhere (or press Space) as fast as you can.
3. Fastest tap wins the round. Tapping early = foul, no point.
4. First to 5 points wins the duel. 2–8 players.
